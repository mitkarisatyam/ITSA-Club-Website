Find Your Place at ITSA.

Whether you're passionate about technology, creativity, operations, finance, or building connections, ITSA offers a community where you can contribute, collaborate, and grow. Applicants must demonstrate leadership, responsibility, and a strong interest in contributing to ITSA.

01

Technical Division

Web Development • AIML • Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) — Full-stack web applications, intelligent systems, and algorithmic problem-solving.

02

Branding Division

Graphics • Media — Creative visual identity, graphic design, photography, video production, and social media storytelling.

03

Operations Division

Operations • Database • Aesthetics — Association logistics, event management, database administration, and venue aesthetics.

04

Relations & Finance Divisions

Industry Relations • Sponsorship • Publicity • Finance — Corporate partnerships, sponsorships, campus publicity, and financial planning.
