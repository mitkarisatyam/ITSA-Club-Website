Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in ITSA events?

All registered engineering students across First Year (FE), Second Year (SE), Third Year (TE), and Final Year (BE) from VIT Pune and affiliated institutions are welcome to register.

Is there any registration fee?

No! All ITSA hackathons, dev challenges, and technical workshops are completely free for students, funded by our association partners and department sponsorship.

Can I register individually for hackathons?

Yes, individual applicants are paired with teammates during the team matching mixer prior to the hackathon kickoff, or you can register your pre-formed squad of 2 to 4 members.

Will attendees receive participation certificates?

Yes, all participants who complete the workshop labs or submit a valid hackathon prototype will receive official, verifiable ITSA VIT Pune certificates.
